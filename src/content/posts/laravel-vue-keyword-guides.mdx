People search for a Laravel and Vue error, not for a stack essay. [State of Laravel 2026](https://stateoflaravel.com/) puts Inertia client rendering at 44.71 percent, up from 37.55 percent in 2025, and Vue reactivity at 51.08 percent. This page maps the guides for that stack by area. Several short pages have been folded into the longer guide they belonged to, so some links below jump to a section.

<TLDR audience="Laravel + Vue developers with a failing request" applies="Laravel 13 · Inertia 3 · Vue 3">

- Guides grouped by area: agents, forms, auth, pages, data, files, jobs and shipping.
- Pick an architecture in the [2026 stack guide](/laravel-vue-in-2026); come here when the app exists and something fails.
- Links with a `#` jump to the section of the longer guide that covers that fix.

</TLDR>

The [2026 stack guide](/laravel-vue-in-2026) is still the page that chooses Inertia, a Sanctum SPA, or Nuxt. Start there when you are picking an architecture. Start here when you already have the app and a failing request.
